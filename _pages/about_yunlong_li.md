## Prof. Yunlong Li

**My M.S. advisor**

I will pursue my M.S. under Prof. Li’s supervision at the **School of Integrated Circuits, Zhejiang University**, with a focus on **advanced IC manufacturing and semiconductor processing**.

Prof. Li’s research spans semiconductor devices, fabrication, integration, and reliability, with particular emphasis on:

- **BEOL-compatible oxide-semiconductor TFTs and monolithic integration**
- **Silicon-based quantum-dot thin-film imaging chips**
- **Advanced packaging and heterogeneous chiplet integration**
- **Silicon CMOS manufacturing processes and reliability**
