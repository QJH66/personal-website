## Prof. Yunlong Li

<p class="advisor-role">Prospective M.S. advisor · Zhejiang University</p>

I will join Prof. Li’s group at the **School of Integrated Circuits, Zhejiang University**, for my master’s studies, focusing on **advanced IC manufacturing and semiconductor processing**.

Prof. Li is a professor and doctoral supervisor whose research focuses on semiconductor devices, fabrication, and integration. His current research interests include:

- **BEOL-compatible oxide thin-film transistors and monolithic integration**
- **Silicon-based quantum-dot thin-film imaging chips**
- **Advanced packaging and heterogeneous chiplet integration**
- **Silicon CMOS fabrication processes and reliability**
